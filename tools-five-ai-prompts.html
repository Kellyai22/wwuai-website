5 AI prompts every woman in business needs to know

These aren't generic prompts. They're built for real business owners who want real results. Copy, tweak and use them today.

01

Content Creation

The Caption Writer

Stop staring at a blank screen. This prompt generates on-brand social captions in seconds. Just swap in your details.

Copy this prompt

"You are a social media copywriter for my business. I run [describe your business] and my audience is [describe your ideal client]. Write 3 Instagram captions for a post about [topic]. Use a warm, encouraging tone. Each caption should be 3–5 sentences and end with a question to spark engagement."

Pro tip: Tell Claude your brand voice once, then reference it in every content prompt. Save it in a note to paste in quickly.

02

Email Writing

The Email Drafter

Whether it's a newsletter, a pitch or a follow-up, Claude can draft it in your voice so you just edit and send.

Copy this prompt

"Write an email to [audience, e.g. my email list or a potential client]. The purpose of the email is [goal, e.g. to share a new offer or check in after a discovery call]. My tone is [e.g. warm, professional, casual]. Keep it under 200 words and end with a clear call to action: [what you want them to do]."

Pro tip: Paste in a previous email you loved and say "match this tone". Claude will mirror your voice almost perfectly.

03

Strategy

The Brainstorm Partner

Stuck on ideas? Claude is the best brainstorm partner you've ever had, and it never gets tired of your questions.

Copy this prompt

"I need content ideas for [platform, e.g. TikTok, Instagram or email]. My business is [brief description] and my audience is [who they are]. Give me 10 content ideas that are practical, relatable and not overly promotional. Focus on ideas that show my expertise without me needing to be on camera."

Pro tip: Ask Claude to give you ideas in different formats, e.g. "give me 3 as reels, 3 as carousels, 4 as text posts."

04

Productivity

The Document Summariser

Long contracts, reports, PDFs: paste them in and get a clear summary in seconds. One of Claude's most useful everyday tools.

Copy this prompt

"Please read the following document and give me: 1) A 3-sentence summary of what it's about. 2) The 5 most important points I need to know. 3) Any actions I need to take or decisions I need to make. Keep your response clear and simple. [Paste document below]"

Pro tip: Works brilliantly with contracts, terms and conditions, course content and long emails. Just paste the text straight in.

05

Decision Making

The Thinking Partner

When you're overthinking a business decision, Claude can help you get out of your head and see things clearly.

Copy this prompt

"I'm trying to decide between [option A] and [option B] for my business. Here's the context: [explain your situation briefly]. Can you help me think through the pros and cons of each? Consider my goal, which is [your goal]. Don't just list facts, help me think through what actually matters here."

Pro tip: Claude won't make the decision for you, but it will surface things you hadn't considered. Treat it like a smart friend, not a magic answer machine.
