What is Wise Women Use AI?+

An AI education community for women in business, founded by Kelly Hartley in Melbourne. Practical, jargon-free training on tools like Claude and ChatGPT, plus a free Skool community where women support each other to actually use AI in their real businesses. There's The Unfair Advantage, the paid membership, when you want the full library.

Who is it for?+

Women entrepreneurs, particularly those in their 40s and 50s, who want to use AI tools in their business but have found the usual content too technical, too salesy, or just not relevant to what they actually do. No coding, no tech background needed.

Do I need to be technical?+

No. The entire approach is built for non-technical business owners. Kelly teaches from the perspective of someone who uses AI as a business tool, not as a developer. If you can write an email, you can learn this.

Which AI tools do you teach?+

Primarily Claude (by Anthropic), with content on ChatGPT as well. The focus is on practical business use: content creation, emails, client communication, workflow automation, and building reusable AI systems inside your business.

Where do I start if I'm not ready to join?+

Start with one of the free resources: Claude, Clearly is a practical guide to getting set up in ten minutes, or grab the AI Starter Kit which includes a Claude comparison tool, five real business prompts, and your first workflow. Both are free, no strings.
