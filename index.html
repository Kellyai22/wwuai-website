An example from Wise Women

One idea into three useful drafts.

A written walkthrough of the content approach used in Wise Women, with sample text so you can see the decisions involved.

01 · The input

Your thinking comes first

A real idea, the person it helps and the facts you can stand behind. Leave private customer details out.

02 · The output

Three useful drafts

A short post, an email opening and a practical tip. Each has a different job and uses the same source.

03 · The human check

You choose what goes out

Check the facts and tone, remove unsupported claims, and save the instructions with the approved version.

Before: rebuild the brief for every draft. After: reuse one source, a clear instruction and a review checklist. Setup and checking still count as work.

What is Wise Women Use AI?

Practical AI and business systems for women running their own businesses, with Gen X women at its heart. Founded by Kelly Hartley in Melbourne, it helps you keep the work that needs your judgement and redesign the rest.

Do I need to be technical?

No coding is needed to start. Begin with a job you understand and a tool you are comfortable using. More involved systems take practice, testing and time to set up.

Where do I find the Business Dependency Diagnostic?

Join the free Wise Women Use AI community on Skool, then open Classroom and choose Business Dependency Diagnostic. If AI is new to you, Start Here and the Claude or ChatGPT foundations are there first. You do not need to complete every lesson before choosing your first improvement.

Which AI tools do you use?

Primarily Claude, with ChatGPT foundations too. The job comes first. Sometimes a checklist or clearer process is more useful than another AI tool.

Architect Advantage is supported DIY implementation. You bring your work, build your version and check its results. Kelly provides the resources, examples and implementation support. Bespoke setup is not included.
