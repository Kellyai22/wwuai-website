Common questions

Do I need to be good at Notion?

No. I drive during the install. You watch and answer questions. The Notion Quick Reference inside your hub covers anything you'll need to do later.

What if I don't have my brand or content fully figured out yet?

The hub helps you figure it out. The Claude prompts inside each section are designed to interview you and pull the answers out. You don't need to come with everything sorted.

Can I do this myself?

Yes. The template is available free inside my Skool community. What you're paying for here is the install. Me sitting with you for 90 minutes and making sure it actually gets done. The same template I gave away free that almost nobody set up.

What happens after the 14 days of support?

You can join the Skool community for ongoing support and a place to ask questions, or upgrade to the AI Content Team install which adds the seven-skill content automation system on top of your hub.

What if it doesn't work for me?

If you complete the install call and decide within 7 days that the hub isn't for you, I'll refund 50% of what you paid. Full refund isn't possible because the work is done in the install call itself.

How long until I can book?

I run a maximum of four installs per month so each client gets proper attention. If founders spots are gone, you'll see a waitlist link on the booking page.
