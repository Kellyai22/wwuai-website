About Kelly

Hey. I'm Kelly.
I built this for us.

Before AI became part of my work, I was managing purchasing, stock, suppliers, people and the recurring jobs that keep a business moving. I know what happens when the answers, the follow-ups and the process all live in one person's head.

I'm British, based in Melbourne, and I built Wise Women Use AI for women running their own businesses, with Gen X women at its heart. We have plenty of experience. What we often need is a better way to carry the work, with room left for life outside it.

One practical example is my newsletter. It starts with my notes, Claude prepares a draft, and I read it, change it and approve it before it goes out. That gives the writing a repeatable starting point while the decisions stay with me. Read how I use AI in my business, or see a worked content example.

I work primarily with Claude and also teach ChatGPT foundations. The tool follows the job. Sometimes the useful change is an AI-assisted draft. Sometimes it is a checklist, a clearer handover or deciding to stop doing something.

Work That Needs You is the starting point: keep your judgement, experience and personal touch where they matter. Build a repeatable way to handle the rest, then test and improve it.

"If you've ever felt like everyone else has figured this out and you're still trying to catch up, you're not behind. You just haven't found the right room yet. This is it."
