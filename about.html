About Kelly

Hey. I'm Kelly.
I built this for us.

I'm British, based in Melbourne, and I run three businesses. I use AI, primarily Claude, as the actual operating system my businesses run on. Not as a demo. Not as an experiment. As the thing that makes it possible to keep going on the days when everything feels like too much.

I started Wise Women Use AI because I couldn't find a space that was honestly useful for women like me. The AI education world is full of people who discovered these tools 18 months ago and started teaching them. Most of it isn't built for women running real businesses, with real energy constraints, in real life.

My focus is Claude, because I know it deeply and I think it's the best tool for most of the work women in business need to do. But I'm not closed off from anything else. I'll always point you toward what's actually going to help, whether that's Claude, another AI tool, or something different entirely. What I won't do is teach you things I haven't tested in my own business first.

I believe women in business deserve to be taken seriously. By the world, by the tools they use, and by themselves. I want every woman who spends time with me to walk away feeling more capable, inspired, and genuinely excited about what's possible for her.

"If you've ever felt like everyone else has figured this out and you're still trying to catch up, you're not behind. You just haven't found the right room yet. This is it."
