Core format

From repeated task to useful AI system

90 minutes · Online or in person

Participants choose one recurring task that takes too much time or lives in one person's head. I guide them through turning it into a clear, repeatable way of working with AI, using examples that fit your audience.

  • A short organiser call to choose the right audience problem and examples
  • A plain-English introduction to using AI safely and sensibly at work
  • A live, hands-on build that people can follow on their own device
  • A practical prompt and worksheet pack to use after the session
  • Time for questions grounded in the work people actually do

The topic and examples are agreed with you before the workshop. No generic tour of every AI tool.

A technical or tool-heavy briefing.

Questions people ask

Before you enquire.

Are the workshops only available in Melbourne?+

I'm based in Melbourne. I can deliver in person locally or online for organisations and business communities across Australia.

What do participants leave with?+

They leave with one AI workflow already started, a practical prompt and worksheet pack, and a clear next step. The exact output depends on the topic we choose together.

Do participants need to be technical?+

No. I teach in plain English and build the session for people using everyday business tools. Confident users still get a practical method they can adapt.

Can you adapt the session for our audience?+

Yes. Before the workshop, we agree who is coming, what they are carrying and what would be genuinely useful. I then adapt the examples and exercises around that.

How much does a workshop cost?+

The investment depends on the format, audience size, preparation and whether the session is online or in person. Email me with the basics and I will send the relevant outline and a clear quote.
