Questions people ask

Before you email.

Do you only work with businesses in Melbourne?+

I'm based in Melbourne, and I love working with local businesses, but nearly all of my work happens over video. I work with women in business right across Australia, so where you live doesn't change what's possible.

What does an AI consultant actually do?+

In my case, something very practical. I look at how your business runs, find the jobs that eat your time, and set up AI systems to take them off your plate. Content, emails, admin, planning. You end up with systems that run, not a strategy document.

Do I need to be technical?+

No. Most of the women I work with wouldn't call themselves technical at all. We set everything up together, in plain English, and you finish knowing how to run it yourself.

Which AI tools do you work with?+

Mainly Claude. It's what I run my own businesses on, so I know it deeply, not just in theory. Where another tool genuinely fits your business better, I'll say so.
