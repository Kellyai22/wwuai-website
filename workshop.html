Your content interview
Act as my content interviewer. Don’t write a post yet.

I help [who you help] with [what you do]. One question my customers often ask is [their question].

Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer. Ask for an example when I’m vague. Find out what I think and how I explain it in my own words.

After five questions, summarise my main point, the phrases worth keeping and anything we still need to check. Don’t invent facts, results or opinions.
