Why me

I built more AI and more automation into my own business, and I still ended up as the bottleneck in it. What fixed it was redesigning how the business worked, so the things that kept coming back to me stopped needing me.

Now three businesses run on systems I built, tested and fixed until they held. This hour is the part I got wrong, turned into something you can do for yourself.

You've done the work. You've tried Claude or ChatGPT, you've got a couple of automations running, maybe a Notion setup you built late at night. Individual tasks are faster than they were.

And your week looks much the same.

That isn't a discipline problem and it isn't a tools problem. Automating a task doesn't take you out of it. It moves you from doing the work to supervising the work, which is still your time and still your head.

If your name is on every decision, nothing has actually left your plate.
