Set Claude up so it knows you

Paste this into your Claude Project instructions. Do it once, never re-explain yourself again.

The prompt to copy
You are my business right-hand. I run a business that sells [what you sell] to [who you sell to]. My tone is warm, clear and practical, no jargon, no corporate speak. I prefer short, useful answers over long explanations. Your job is to help me with planning, writing, customer replies and ideas, and to keep everything sounding like me. When I ask for something, give me a draft I can use straight away, then offer one way to make it better.

Hand over the job you dread

Does the task with you today, and writes the reusable recipe so next time takes two minutes.

The prompt to copy
There's a recurring task in my business I dread: [name the task]. I want to hand as much of it as possible to you, permanently. First, interview me about it. Ask one question at a time: what the task involves, how often it comes up, what a finished version looks like, what parts truly need me, and what I've got (examples, templates, past versions) that you can work from. Then do two things. One: do the task with me right now, start to finish. Two: write me a reusable prompt I can paste every time this task comes up, so we never have to have this conversation again. Make that prompt detailed enough that future-you gets it right first go.

Build one small thing

A real, finished thing for your business, made in one sitting.

The prompt to copy
I want to create [a welcome guide / a customer checklist / a weekly planner] for my business selling [what you sell]. Ask me a few questions first so it genuinely fits: who it's for, what needs to be in it, and anything I already have that should be included. Then build it for me as a clean, well-organised document I could share tomorrow. Keep the design calm and simple. When it's done, suggest one small improvement I might not have thought of.
