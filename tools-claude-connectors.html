Asks to try

Claude, connected

Once Claude is connected to your Gmail and calendar, these are the asks worth trying first. Copy one, paste it into Claude, see what comes back.

0 of 6 used
01

The inbox short list

Gmail. Turns the pile into the handful that actually needs you.

What emails am I sitting on that actually need a reply this week? Give me a short list, most important first, with one line each on why.
02

Pick up the thread

Gmail. For the conversation you lost track of.

Find the thread with [name] about [topic] and summarise where we left it, what they're waiting on from me, and what I should say next.
03

The warm reply

Gmail. Drafts it, you check it, you send it.

Draft a warm reply to [name]'s most recent email. Keep it short, sound like me, and end with a clear next step.
04

The week, honestly

Calendar. See the shape of the week before it lands on you.

Look at my calendar for the coming week and tell me honestly which day is going to hurt. Where are my only real work blocks? What can I prepare tonight in ten minutes to soften the worst day?
05

Tomorrow, briefed

Calendar. Walk in knowing what each thing is.

What am I walking into tomorrow? Give me a two-line brief for each thing on my calendar: what it is, and the one thing I should have ready.
06

The fresh-chat handover

For when a conversation gets long and Claude asks for a new chat. Nothing gets lost.

This conversation is getting long. Please write me a handover summary I can paste into a fresh chat: what we're working on, every decision we've made, where we got to, and the very next step. Keep it complete enough that the new chat can pick up without asking me anything.

These cards are the floor, not the ceiling. Change any of them until they sound like you, and bring what you make back to the community. We love a show and tell.
