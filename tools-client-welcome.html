Welcome a new client without starting from scratch

Prepare a welcome email, useful intake questions and an internal checklist from the offer and process you already have.

Claude or ChatGPT · One offer · Drafts to check before use

Start with the offer you actually deliver

A new client has said yes. They need to know what happens next, and you need the information that lets you start. Build that small handover before adding more tools.

  • Your current offer and confirmed inclusions.
  • The steps you already take after someone becomes a client.
  • The details you need from them, the documents you already use and any real start dates.
  • The questions new clients keep asking.

Prepare the welcome pack

Client welcome pack
Help me prepare a reusable welcome process for one offer.

Offer and confirmed inclusions: [details]
What confirms someone is ready to start: [for example signed agreement and confirmed payment]
Current steps and tools: [what really happens]
Information needed from the client: [what is necessary]
Confirmed timing and responsibilities: [details]
Existing emails, forms or checklists: [paste useful material]

First identify essential missing information and ask up to five short questions. Then prepare:
1. A short welcome email explaining what happens next, what I need from the client and how to ask a question.
2. Only the intake questions needed to deliver this offer. Explain why each is needed and flag any sensitive information to collect through an appropriate secure route instead of this chat.
3. An internal checklist: trigger, action, owner, timing, completion check and what to do if something is missing.
4. A reusable template with clear placeholders, plus one filled example using only the details I supplied.

Reuse existing wording and assets where they work. Do not invent inclusions, payment status, dates, policies or promises. Label unresolved details "confirm before use". Do not send anything, charge anyone, create live forms or change permissions. Finish with a test of the proposed steps using a fictional client.

Try a fictional client first

Worked example · fictional business

Offer: A 60-minute website review with a written summary. The client supplies their website address and one main question. The review date has not been booked.

Useful welcome: Confirm what is included, ask for the website address and question, and explain the real booking step.

Incorrect welcome: Promise a redesigned website next Friday. Neither the work nor the deadline is in the offer.

Walk through the welcome process from the client’s side. Can they open the links? Is it clear what they need to provide? Does each completed step lead to the next? Use draft materials and a test record.

Save the version you can repeat

  • Replace or resolve every placeholder before using the pack. Check the offer, timing and client details.
  • Save the approved welcome email, intake questions and checklist with the offer they belong to.
  • If you build a form or connect your tools later, test that setup separately. The prompt creates drafts, not a working automation.
  • Review the pack after the first client uses it. Add the explanation they still needed.

You are finished when: the pack matches the offer and a test client can follow the steps without guessing. For the broader process, return to Build the system behind the sale.

Put one piece to work

Try it on a real job. If a step gets stuck, bring that step and what happened to the free community. Leave out private details.
