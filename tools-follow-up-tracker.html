Know who needs a follow-up this week

Use one simple list to keep enquiries, next actions and follow-up dates from disappearing into your inbox.

Your existing sheet or CRM · Claude or ChatGPT for the weekly review

Put the open conversations in one place

A well-written follow-up helps only if you remember who needs it. Start with the enquiries already open, using a simple sheet or table. You do not need to change your customer-management tool.

Record a reference or name, enquiry, current status, last contact, next action, next date, owner and a link to the original conversation. Include a “closed / do not contact” status. Keep contact details in your existing system where possible.

Review the list once a week

Weekly follow-up review
Help me review my open enquiries. Use only this table.

Today's date and timezone: [date and timezone]
My follow-up rules: [how I normally follow up, any promised dates, and when I stop]
Enquiry table: [paste current rows, including status, last contact, next action, next date, owner and source reference]

List:
1. Actions due or overdue, in date order.
2. Open enquiries missing a next action, owner or date.
3. Items waiting for my decision.
4. Closed, declined or do-not-contact items to exclude from follow-up.

For missing dates, ask me or label a suggested date for approval. Do not invent agreement, interest or a response. If a row is contradictory, flag it.

Before suggesting a send, remind me to check the original conversation for a newer reply. Do not contact anyone, update my live records or restart a closed enquiry. Return the exact rows that need a decision and why.

Close the loop

Check the conversation, then use the existing follow-up writing prompt where a message is still needed. After you send, record the date and the next action. When the person replies, update the status immediately.

  • Choose a weekly review slot in your real calendar. The table does not remind you by itself.
  • Keep one current tracker. If you already have a CRM, use these fields and checks there.
  • Close enquiries that are finished, declined or no longer appropriate to chase.

You are finished when: every open enquiry has an owner and a next action with a date, or a clearly stated reason for waiting. The next review is in your calendar.
