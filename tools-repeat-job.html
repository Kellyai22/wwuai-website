Explain a repeat job once

Turn a real explanation into a checklist someone else can follow, then test it on the work it is meant to support.

Claude or ChatGPT · One recurring job · No new software required

Choose a job you can demonstrate

If somebody asked how you prepare a client folder, you could probably explain it. The difficulty is remembering every small step when you write instructions. Use a real run of the job as your source.

  • Pick one recurring job with a clear beginning and end.
  • Talk through it or write rough notes as you do it. Include the information you use and how you know the result is right.
  • Remove passwords and private customer details. Include a safe example of the finished work.

Turn the explanation into a procedure

Repeat-job checklist
Help me turn one repeated business job into instructions another person could follow.

The job: [one specific task]
What starts it: [request, date or event]
My rough explanation: [steps in my own words or a transcript]
Tools and information used: [what is needed]
Example of a good result: [describe or attach a safe example]
What still needs my judgement: [decisions I keep]

First ask up to five questions about missing steps, one at a time. Do not invent how my tools work or claim to have used them.

Then write a short procedure containing:
1. The trigger, responsible person and finished result.
2. The inputs and where the person finds them, without passwords.
3. Numbered actions in the actual order, each with a visible check.
4. Decisions that need my approval and when to stop and ask.
5. Common exceptions supported by my notes, and any unknowns.
6. A final checklist and where the completed work is saved.

Separate what a person does from what AI can draft or organise. Flag a possible automation as a suggestion, not something already running. Finish with the smallest real test we can do.

Test the instructions

Worked example · fictional business

Job: Prepare a folder for a new design client once the agreement is confirmed.

Useful steps: Confirm the agreement status. Copy the approved folder structure. Add the supplied brief. Check the client name and access list. Ask the owner to approve access before inviting anyone.

Exception: If the brief is missing, request it using the approved wording. Do not create a fictional brief to finish the checklist.

Run the procedure on one real job yourself, following only the written instructions. If you have a helper, ask them to try it and note where they have to ask you a question.

Fix the step that still needs explaining

Procedure correction
We tested the procedure. Here is where it broke or needed explanation:
[What happened, the missing information and what we actually did.]

Revise only the affected steps. Preserve the approval points. List the changes and anything still untested. Return a clean procedure with a version date and a short final checklist.

You are finished when: the job has been completed using the procedure, the result passes your checks and the remaining judgement calls are clearly assigned. Save the current version in your business headquarters or the shared place you already use. Review it when the process changes.
