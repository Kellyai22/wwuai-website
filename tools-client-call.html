Turn a client call into actions and a follow-up

Leave the call with a clear record of decisions, responsibilities and the questions that still need answering.

Claude or ChatGPT · Your notes or an authorised transcript

Start with notes you can trust

The call is finished, but the decisions are scattered through your notes. Give AI the notes so it can organise them. You still check what was agreed before the follow-up leaves your outbox.

  • Use your own notes or a transcript you are authorised to use. Remove unrelated private details.
  • Include the meeting date, timezone where dates matter, and the roles or names needed to understand the actions.
  • Have the original notes available for checking. A summary cannot recover an agreement that was never recorded.

Turn the call into actions

Call notes and follow-up
Turn these meeting notes into a practical record and a draft follow-up email.

Meeting date and timezone: [date and timezone]
People and roles: [who attended]
Purpose: [why we met]
Notes or transcript: [paste source]
My preferred email tone and sign-off: [short example]

Return:
1. Confirmed decisions, each with a short supporting quote or source reference.
2. Actions in a table: task, owner, due date, source and status.
3. Open questions and suggestions that were discussed but not agreed.
4. A short email confirming the decisions, responsibilities and next step.

Use only the notes. Where an owner or deadline was not agreed, write "needs confirmation". Convert relative dates only if the meeting date and wording make them unambiguous; otherwise ask. Do not treat an idea or a request as a commitment. Do not invent a price, scope change or delivery promise.

The email is a draft for me to check. Do not send messages or create calendar events or tasks. Highlight contradictions or missing details before the draft.

Keep an unanswered question visible

Worked example · fictional business

Notes: “Mia will send the current logo on Friday, 16 October 2026. Kelly will review it. We discussed adding a second page, but didn’t agree on scope or price.”

Action: Mia sends the logo by 16 October. Kelly’s review date needs confirmation.

Open question: The extra page and its price remain undecided.

Email line: “Once the logo arrives, I’ll confirm the review date. The second page is still a possibility; we haven’t agreed the scope or price.”

Check it against the call

  • Check decisions and tasks against the source, especially names, dates, scope and money.
  • Confirm missing owners and deadlines instead of accepting a plausible guess.
  • Edit and send the email yourself after checking the recipient and attachments.
  • Put confirmed actions into the task list you already use. Keep a link to the meeting record.

You are finished when: the checked record is saved, confirmed actions have a home, and the follow-up is ready for your approval. For the next call, reuse the prompt with new notes.

Put one piece to work

Try it on a real job. If a step gets stuck, bring that step and what happened to the free community. Leave out private details.
