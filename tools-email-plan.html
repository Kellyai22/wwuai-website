One-email starter
Help me draft one useful email for my readers.

Who I help: [your audience]
One thing I want to explain: [your idea or a real customer question]
The facts or advice I can support: [your notes]
A few sentences that sound like me: [your own writing]
One useful next step for the reader: [reply, try something or visit a verified link]

Write one short email with a clear subject line and one main point. Use my words as a guide and Australian English. Do not invent personal stories, results, prices or deadlines. Ask if a missing detail matters. Finish by listing anything I should check. This is a draft for me to review, not permission to send.

Check before sending

Read it aloud, correct every fact, check the link and remove anything you would not say. Paste the checked draft into your usual email platform and send yourself a test. Keep your final version as an example for next time.
