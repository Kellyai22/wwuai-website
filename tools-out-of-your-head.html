Out of Your Head

The four habits from the course, as copy-and-keep cards. These ones work the same for every business, so nothing to fill in. Just copy, paste, breathe out.

01

The catch-all sort

Turns your messy caught list into three calm piles and tomorrow’s three.

Here's my catch-all list from today, exactly as I wrote it, mess and all: [paste your list]. Please sort it into three groups for me: things that genuinely need me to do them, things that can wait and when they should resurface, and things that can quietly go. Be honest about that last group. Then give me tomorrow's three, the only three things worth my energy tomorrow. Keep it warm and brief, I'm reading this tired.
02

The 9pm brain dump

Two minutes before you wind down. Hands back a short list and permission to stop.

It's the end of my day and my head is full. I'm going to empty it here, in no particular order, and I need three things from you. One: tomorrow's short list, no more than three things, based on what actually matters in all this. Two: anything in here that's a worry rather than a task, name it kindly and park it, worries don't belong on to-do lists. Three: tell me plainly that the rest is caught and safe and I can stop for tonight. Here's everything: [empty your head here]
03

The three piles

What only you can do, what Claude can carry, and what no one needs to do at all.

I want to sort what I'm carrying into three piles: what genuinely needs me, what you could carry for me, and what no one needs to do at all. Interview me gently, one question at a time. Ask what I'm carrying at work and at home, what I'd never give up, what I dread, and what I do only because it feels expected. Push back kindly if you catch me defending something that sounds like habit rather than need. Then show me the three piles, and be brave about the third one. Finish with the single best first thing to hand over, and why.
04

The Sunday reset

Ten minutes, cuppa in hand. Your week on one screen before Monday decides for you.

Help me look at my week before it starts. Here's what's on, work and home together: [list what you know is coming, appointments, deadlines, family things, the lot]. Please: lay it out day by day so I can see the shape of it, flag the heaviest day and suggest what to move or drop, tell me what I could prepare in ten minutes now that would save me an hour mid-week, and name the one thing I should protect time for because it matters most. Keep the whole answer short enough to screenshot.

These cards are the floor, not the ceiling. Change any of them until they sound like you, and bring what you make back to the community. We love a show and tell.
