A few quick questions

Do I need Notion?

Yes, and the free plan is plenty. The whole thing arrives as a Notion template you duplicate in one click. If you have never used Notion, the pack walks you through the only three things you actually need to know.

Do I need to pay for AI?

The prompts work with the free version of Claude or ChatGPT. A paid plan makes longer sessions easier, but nothing here requires one.

Will it sound like me, or like AI?

That is what the voice brief is for. You build it once from three things you have already written, and every prompt uses it. You will still read what comes back and change lines, and you should. Anything that promises writing you never have to touch is selling you something that will not sound like you.

Is this the same as what you teach in the membership?

No, and deliberately not. In the membership you build your own system over the month, guided, one step at a time. The Pack hands you ninety days already written to adapt. If you would rather learn to do it yourself, the membership is the better buy.

What if my business is nothing like yours?

The calendar is built on four pillar types that work for almost any business, and the remap prompt relabels the whole thing to your own pillars in about ten minutes. It also works out your pillars first if you do not know them yet.

How long until I can actually post something?

An afternoon, if you sit down with it. Fifteen minutes on the voice brief, ten on the remap, then you are picking a framework and filling in the brackets.

Do I have to post four times a week?

No. It is built at four because that is a rhythm most people can keep on a bad week, and a calendar you abandon in week three is worth nothing. Do two if two is what you have.
