The things you might be wondering

A few honest answers.

Do I need to be technical?
No. The point is to make the business work better, not turn you into a developer. Some builds use ordinary Claude or ChatGPT. Others include an optional Claude Code version when that genuinely makes the system better.
How much time will I need?
Enough to choose the problem, build a first version and test it on real work. That will vary by system, but this is designed around small implementation steps rather than hours of lessons.
What happens after I join?
Start with Welcome to Architect Advantage. Choose one bottleneck, then choose a relevant lesson from the existing library. Do not try to work through the whole classroom.
Is this all about automation?
No. Sometimes the right answer is AI. Sometimes it is a checklist, a template, a clearer decision or stopping the job altogether. We choose the system after we understand the work.
Will you build the system for me?
No. I give you the path, tools, examples and support, but you build your version. That matters because you need to understand it when the business changes.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Architect Advantage is billed monthly through Skool and you can cancel at any time.
What if I am not ready to pay?
Join the free Wise Women Use AI community first. You can learn the foundations, find your first bottleneck and make a useful first change before deciding whether you want the paid implementation path.
