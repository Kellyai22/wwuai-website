Wise Women Use AI · Mental Model

Think of AI as
your business team

The fastest way to start using AI effectively is to stop thinking of it as a search engine, and start thinking of it as a team of specialists on call 24/7.

The old way of thinking

"I'll ask AI a question and see what it says."

This leads to generic, unhelpful answers, and the feeling that AI isn't really that useful for your business.

The better mental model

"I'm briefing a skilled team member on a specific task."

This leads to precise, useful outputs, and the feeling that you have a capable team behind you.

How to brief your team

The better your brief, the better the output. Think of every prompt as a briefing document.

Your role
→
The director. You set the goal, context and standards. AI does the execution.
Context
→
Tell Claude who you are, who you serve, and what tone you use. Do this once per session.
The task
→
Be specific: what exactly do you need, in what format, for what purpose?
The standard
→
Tell Claude what good looks like. Length, tone, examples, what to avoid.
Feedback
→
Not right? Tell it exactly what to change, like you would a real team member. It won't be offended.

The mindset shifts that change everything

Instead of thinking… Think…
"I'll ask it a quick question" "I'm briefing a specialist on a task"
"The first answer is the final answer" "This is a first draft, I'll refine it"
"AI will replace my voice and ideas" "AI amplifies my voice and executes my ideas"
"If the output is bad, AI doesn't work" "If the output is bad, my brief needs work"
"I need to learn all of this before I start" "I learn by doing, start now, refine as I go"
"AI is a tech thing, not for me" "AI is a business tool, built for anyone with work to do"
