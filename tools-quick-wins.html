AI quick wins you can use today

No setup, no tech skills, no learning curve. These are the fastest ways to get real value from AI in your business, starting right now.

Content 2 minutes

Repurpose a post you've already written

Take something you already created and turn it into a different format instantly.

"Take this Instagram caption and turn it into a short LinkedIn post. Keep the same message but make the tone slightly more professional. [paste caption]"

One piece of content becomes two in minutes

Admin 3 minutes

Summarise a long email thread

Paste in a long email chain and get the key points without reading every word.

"Here is an email thread. Give me: 1) what it's about in one sentence, 2) what I need to do next, 3) any deadlines mentioned. [paste thread]"

Never re-read a long email chain again

Communications 5 minutes

Write a tricky email you've been avoiding

That email you keep putting off? Claude will draft it in 30 seconds.

"Write an email to [person] about [situation]. I want to [goal, for example: decline politely, follow up without being pushy, or ask for a discount]. Keep it short, warm, and professional."

Off your to-do list, done professionally

Content 10 minutes

Plan a week of content in one go

Stop thinking about what to post each day. Plan it all at once and batch your work.

"I run [business type] for [audience]. Give me 5 social media post ideas for this week. Mix formats: 2 tips, 1 personal story angle, 1 question post, 1 promotion. Keep topics relevant to [your niche]."

A full week of ideas in one sitting

Strategy 10 minutes

Get unstuck on a business decision

When you're going in circles in your own head, an outside perspective changes everything.

"I'm stuck on [decision]. Here's my situation: [context]. What are the most important things to consider? What am I probably not thinking about? Help me think this through clearly."

Clarity without the overthinking spiral

Admin 15 minutes

Create a simple FAQ for your business

Save yourself from answering the same questions over and over. Build it once.

"I run [business]. Write a FAQ section with 8 common questions my customers or clients might ask, and clear helpful answers. Base it on this information about my business: [brief description of what you do and offer]."

A FAQ page ready to copy straight onto your website

Content 20 minutes

Write your About page or bio

One of the hardest things to write about yourself. Let Claude do the heavy lifting.

"Write an About page for my business website. Here are the facts about me and my business: [brief bullet points]. My audience is [who they are]. The tone should be [warm/professional/conversational]. Keep it under 200 words and make it feel personal, not corporate."

A finished About page in one session

Strategy 25 minutes

Build a simple offer description

Struggling to explain what you sell in a way that lands? Claude can help you find the right words.

"Help me write a clear, compelling description of my offer. Here's what it is: [describe your product or service]. The person buying it wants to [outcome they want]. Their main worry is [their biggest concern]. Write 3 versions: a one-liner, a short paragraph, and a 5-bullet summary."

Three ready-to-use versions of your offer description
