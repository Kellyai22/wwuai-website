What we build together

01 Week

Teach Claude who you are

You already have what Claude needs. It lives in your about page, your old conversations, your brain. We collect it, shape it, and load it into a Claude Project that knows your voice, your audience, your offers, and the things you'd never say. 02 Week

Your content strategy

Not a complicated framework. Not a content calendar with seventeen columns.

A simple structure. Three or four content pillars that reflect what your business actually does and what your audience actually needs. A posting rhythm that fits your real life, not someone else's. Once this is in your Claude Project, you stop deciding what to post. You just start.

03 Week

The weekly workflow

This is where you feel it working.

From rough thinking on Sunday to scheduled content by Wednesday. You run the full workflow during this module with your real business, producing your actual content for that week. Not a practice run. 04 Week

Making it sound like you

The fear is real. "But it won't sound like me." This module answers it properly.

05 Week

Making it stick

You've had the Week 3 feeling before. The energy that doesn't last. This module builds the structure that makes it last anyway.

Your minimal weekly routine. Your batching option for harder weeks. The checklists and references that mean you never have to reinvent it. The system runs because you've designed it to run. Not because you remembered to.
